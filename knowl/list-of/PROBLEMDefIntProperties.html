Problem 20.4.0.5 Properties of the Definite Integral.

This problem is very illuminative and will help you develop your intuition about definite integrals. You should do it—or at least give it serious thought—whether your instructor has assigned it or not.
🔗
Give an example of a finite sum which displays each of properties \(1\) through \(6\) in Table 20.4.0.3, and show that each property is true for finite sums.
🔗
Use the “way of thinking” about definite integrals described above.
🔗
Hint.
For Property 6 notice that the sum not the left goes from left (lesser) to right (greater) while the sum on the right goes from right to left. What impact does that have on \(\dx{x}\text{?}\)
